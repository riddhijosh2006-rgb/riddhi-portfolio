About

I'm a final-year B.Tech Computer Science / Data Science student. My focus sits at the intersection of Data Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Backend Development — I like understanding how data actually moves: from source, through processing, into storage, and out the other side as something people can read on a dashboard.

Most of what I know comes from building things myself — pipelines that pull from APIs, load into PostgreSQL, and get reshaped with SQL; a hospital management backend with role-based access; a churn model evaluated the way it should be, not the way that looks best in a slide. I'm not claiming years of production experience — I'm claiming that I build, break, and rebuild things until I understand them.
